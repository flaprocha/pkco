# PKCO Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how PKCO handles your information. PKCO is developed by Felipe Rocha.

## What PKCO is

PKCO (Personal Knowledge and Context Organizer) is an offline notes app for Android. You can write text notes, give each note one category and add tags, then search and filter your notes. The app works without a network connection.

## What the app stores

The app stores only what you enter and the times your notes were changed:

- the title and body of each note;
- your categories and tags;
- when each note was created and last edited.

This information is kept only in a local database in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

## What the app does not do

- PKCO does not collect, send, share or sell any information. Your notes never leave your phone through the app.
- There is no account and no sign-in.
- The app does not have the INTERNET permission, so it cannot connect to the internet.
- The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party data-collection or tracking SDKs. It is built with Android's own Jetpack (AndroidX) libraries and the Kotlin libraries.
- The app does not ask for any permission that gives it access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or other files on your phone.

## Backup and moving to a new phone

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for PKCO. Your notes are not copied to a cloud backup, and they do not move to a new phone when you transfer your data.

## Deleting your data

- Deleting a note removes it permanently. It cannot be recovered.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone.

## Security

PKCO does not add its own encryption. Your notes are protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps each app's private storage separate, and by your phone's screen lock and storage encryption. Use a screen lock to protect your notes.

## Children

PKCO is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of PKCO collects any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The date at the top shows when the policy was last changed.
